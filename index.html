Nutritionist's notes

  • These are general endurance-sports starting points, not individual medical advice — dial them in during training, never for the first time on race day.
  • Carb tolerance is trainable. If 90g+/hr is new to you, build up to it in long training sessions first.
  • Sodium needs vary a lot between individuals and conditions — a sweat-sodium test gives a far more precise number than any generic range.
  • Let fluid intake track thirst and urine colour on the day — overdrinking against a fixed target can be as risky as underdrinking.
